In a world overrun by relentless monsters, survival demands more than strength — it demands sacrifice. *Sacrivive* is a fast-paced shooter where every battle is a brutal test of courage and strategy. As the last surviving defender of humanity, you wield a devastating power: the ability to sacrifice your own health to unleash explosive burst attacks that can annihilate waves of enemies.

But every choice comes with a cost. Push yourself too far, and you risk falling before the monsters do. Hold back, and you might be overwhelmed. Master the balance between self-preservation and self-destruction as you fight through hordes of nightmarish creatures, upgrade your abilities, and uncover the dark truth behind the invasion.
